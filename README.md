# **Credit Risk & Loan Default Analysis**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **32,581 loans** portfolio to identify high-risk borrower and loan segments, understand factors associated with loan defaults, and evaluate where the portfolio has the greatest financial exposure.

The analysis was performed using **SQL** on a credit risk dataset. The project focuses on:

- Identifying high-risk loan segments
- Understanding factors associated with loan defaults
- Measuring portfolio exposure
- Comparing defaulted and non-defaulted borrowers
- Identifying segments with high risk and significant financial exposure
- Generating business insights for credit risk monitoring

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

**How can a lending institution identify high-risk loan segments, understand the key factors associated with loan defaults, and reduce credit risk across its loan portfolio?**
---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **32,581 loans** with information related to borrowers and their loans.
